Why deterministic workflows matter

Audit preparation workflows require traceable and reviewable outputs — not opaque autonomous generation.

Most AI-based compliance tools produce probabilistic outputs without explicit evidence linkage. For QMS audit preparation, that creates unacceptable review burden and traceability gaps.

Probabilistic / opaque systems

  • Confidence scores without evidence basis
  • Hallucination risk in finding generation
  • Uncontrolled output variation
  • Cannot trace finding to source signal

ZEXLmed approach

  • Every verdict linked to extracted evidence
  • Deterministic — same input, same output
  • Explicit clause mapping per finding
  • Designed for human review workflow
How it works

From document to structured finding

Submit a QMS document. The system performs structured evidence intake, clause-aligned evaluation, and generates a traceable finding for human review.

01 — INTAKE
Structured evidence intake
Document type classification routes clause applicability logic. CAPA Record, Audit Record, Preventive Action, Supplier Record — each follows a distinct evaluation path.
02 — EVALUATE
Clause-aligned evaluation
Clause-relevant signals extracted — root cause investigation, effectiveness verification, scope statements, CRQ references — mapped to applicable ISO / QMSR requirements.
03 — DETECT
Deterministic finding generation
Gap signals identified and aggregated into a 4-level verdict with finding rationale, trigger pattern, and basis evidence. No probabilistic scoring.
04 — REVIEW
Human review workflow
Output is structured for QA/RA review — not autonomous compliance determination. Findings include full traceability for reviewer verification and escalation.
Verdict system

Four-level deterministic output

Every evaluation produces one of four verdicts, each backed by structured finding rationale, trigger pattern, and clause mapping — designed for reviewer action.

COMPLIANT
All required elements present. Evidence of procedure, execution, and closure mapped to applicable clauses.
MINOR_GAP
Incomplete but not deficient. Procedural steps present with isolated documentation gaps — no systemic failure signal.
NOT_COMPLIANT
Required elements absent or deficient. Root cause, effectiveness review, or mandatory linkages missing from expected structure.
CRITICAL_FINDING
Systemic or recurrent failure signal. Triggers on recurrence without CAPA linkage, effectiveness absence, or blank mandatory sections.
