1. Overview

ZEXLmed ("we," "us," or "our") operates the compliance analysis platform at zexlmed.com. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information in connection with our Service.

2. Information We Collect

Account and Access Information:

  • Email address (for invite code requests and service communications)

Document Submissions:

  • QMS documents you upload for analysis (.docx files)

3. No PHI Collection

The Service is not designed to collect or process protected health information (PHI) as defined under HIPAA. Users must not submit documents containing PHI. If we detect or are notified of PHI in submitted documents, we will delete such documents promptly and notify the submitting user.

4. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and operate the Service
  • Generate and deliver compliance analysis outputs
  • Maintain service security and prevent abuse

  • Communicate with users regarding Service updates and access

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or document content with third parties for marketing or commercial purposes.

5. Document Retention

Documents submitted for analysis are processed to generate outputs and are not permanently retained. Documents and associated analysis data may be retained for a limited period (not to exceed 90 days) for quality assurance and accuracy improvement purposes, after which they are deleted.

You may request deletion of your submitted documents at any time by contacting us at hello@zexlmed.com.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information submitted to the Service, including:

  • Encrypted transmission (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of submitted information.

The Service is hosted on Railway infrastructure. Your use of the Service is subject to Railway's applicable data processing terms. We do not integrate advertising networks or third-party analytics that access your document content.

8. Cookies and Tracking

The Service uses minimal session-based technical mechanisms necessary for service operation. We do not use persistent tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics that profile individual users.
